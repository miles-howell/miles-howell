### Hi, I'm Miles

I enjoy working on low-level problems, mainly in AI and cybersecurity. In highschool, while OpenAI was still focused on Gym, I wrote a [single-neuron network](https://github.com/mileshowell/Artificial-Intelligence/blob/master/fruit_classifier.py) and reworked [Karpathy's famous min-char-rnn](https://github.com/mileshowell/Artificial-Intelligence/blob/master/rnn.py) to show off to classmates.

Also in highschool, I co-lead the programming club and consulted for the state's first Cybersecurity high school cirriculum.

In college, I spent 2 years doing coursework in Computer Science and Mathematics. During my time there, I started and lead the ML/AI Special Interest Group (SIG). This was an extracurricular activity that was optional to anyone at the school. At the time, there were no undergraduate ML/AI courses available and only a handful of graduate-level courses available. In the sessions, I broke down complex topics in ML using plain English and no high-level libraries.

The goal was not to build or design a product or tool, but to understand the technology at its core. High-level libraries outsource your understanding! Obviously things have changed in AI development, and writing a production codebase of any kind with no libraries would be insanity. But this time was invaluable to me in that I fully understood the concepts behind machine learning at the time.

Personally and currently I'm interested in the frontier of AI research and experimenting with [new implemenations](https://github.com/miles-howell/Bridge-Crossing-RL-Sim). I've gotten incredibly bored with the big AI ~~labs~~ companies and their current pace of research. If not pace, then style and focus of their work. I miss the days when anything was possible with AI!! Now, we only call it a "breakthrough" if it means my AI waifu uses less tokens to tell me she loves me. 

*Really* though, I'm just exhausted of LLMs and the news cycles. There are much *MUCH* more interesting (and world-altering) ways to use this technology. We just started to scratch the surface when ChatGPT and its counterparts appeared and forever changed the landscape of ML research, skewing everyone's perspective towards a narrow use case of a technology that's existed for nearly century!
