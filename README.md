### Hi, I'm Miles

I enjoy working on low-level problems, mainly in AI and cybersecurity. In highschool, while OpenAI was still focused on Gym, I wrote a [single-neuron network](https://github.com/mileshowell/Artificial-Intelligence/blob/master/fruit_classifier.py) and reworked [Karpathy's famous min-char-rnn](https://github.com/mileshowell/Artificial-Intelligence/blob/master/rnn.py) to show off to classmates.

Also in highschool, I co-lead the programming club and consulted for the state's first Cybersecurity high school cirriculum.

In college, I spent 2 years doing coursework in Computer Science and Mathematics. During my time there, I started and lead the ML/AI Special Interest Group (SIG). This was an extracurricular activity that was optional to anyone at the school. At the time, there were no undergraduate ML/AI courses available and only a handful of graduate courses available. I lead the sessions by breaking down complex topics in ML in plain english and at the lowest possible level. High-level libraries outsource your understanding! Things have obviously changed but this time was invaluable in fully understanding the concepts behind machine learning.

Now I support businesses by modernizing legacy applications and tools. Check out my pinned repos for some of the cooler projects.

Personally and currently I'm interested in the frontier of AI research and experimenting with [new implemenations](https://github.com/miles-howell/Bridge-Crossing-RL-Sim). I've gotten incredibly bored with the big 3-4 AI companies and their current pace of research. If not pace, then style and focus of their work. I miss the days when anything was possible with AI!! Now, we only call it a "breakthrough" if it means my AI waifu uses less tokens to tell me she loves me. 

*Really* though, I'm just tired (*exhausted*) of LLMs and brain-dead news cycles about "improvements". There are much *MUCH* more interesting (and world-altering) ways to use this technology. We just started to scratch the surface when ChatGPT and its counterparts appeared and forever changed the landscape of ML research, skewing everyone's perspective towards a narrow use case of a technology that's existed for nearly century!
